## Claim sourcing

Every claim in the film comes from ZeroTaken's own site:

- "only shows names you can actually register" — FAQ, *What makes ZeroTaken
  different*: "only shows domains that are actually available to register at
  standard registrar pricing. We filter out parked, premium-priced, and
  aftermarket listings."
- "240+ extensions", "registry direct, no cache" — FAQ, *Which domain
  extensions can I search* and *Does ZeroTaken front-run*.
- "scored for brandability" — the Instant Domain Quality Score section
  ("length, pronounceability, brandability and memorability", "92 · Excellent").
- "the handle, everywhere" — the social handles section (YouTube, Facebook,
  Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest).
- "searches never leave your browser", "no front-running" — FAQ, *Does
  ZeroTaken front-run…*: "saved directly in your browser's local storage… no
  domain front-running or broker snipers."
- "free to search" — FAQ, *Is ZeroTaken free to use*.

The `$2,450` figure in beat 3 is illustrative of the premium-pricing trap the
site describes; the testimonial on the site cites `$2,000`. Swap it for the
testimonial's figure if you want it strictly quoted.
